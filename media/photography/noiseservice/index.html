Noise Service — album release

A raw and emotive set from the night Noise Service released their debut album. Founded in Seattle in 2021, the band folds shoegaze, retro rock, and Latin influence into what they call kaleidoscopic fusion rock — Andy Mowdy, Corey Ray Zimmerman, Katharine McAllister, and Rolando Sotomayor Pino. Visit their site or hear the debut album.
