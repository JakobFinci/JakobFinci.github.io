Introduction

Eosinophilic Esophagitis (EoE) is a chronic immune-mediated disorder of the esophagus. This review surveys established and emerging therapies, from dietary interventions and PPIs to biologics in clinical trials.

Key Findings

  • Dupilumab is changing the treatment landscape for moderate-to-severe disease.
  • Anti-IL-5 agents reduce eosinophils but may not improve symptoms proportionally.
  • Long-term safety and efficacy data for investigational agents remains a major gap.
