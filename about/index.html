Index 04 — About

About

I'm Elias — also Eliyahu — Suskind, and I work across engineering, the life sciences, and visual media. I'm most at home where those fields overlap: writing software and building hardware, doing biological and biochemical research, and making images. The through-line is a habit of treating creative and technical problems the same way — carefully, and with an eye for how the pieces fit together.

Academic & research direction

My path through school has been deliberately broad. I started in product development at Babson, moved into electrical and computer engineering at Olin College, and now study biology — neurobiology and physiology — as an undergraduate at Arizona State University while working toward a dual M.S. in computer science and microbiology at Oregon State University. My published research reflects that range, from the biochemistry of post-fermented tea to a clinical review of therapies for eosinophilic esophagitis. You can read those in Research.

Technical background

My engineering background sits at the intersection of hardware, software, and scientific computing. I like building tools that make data legible — the tea study, for instance, leaned as much on a Python analysis pipeline as it did on lab assays. Alongside research I do freelance web and localization work, currently for the beverage company Goko Energy.

Creative practice

I photograph as one half of the duo Golden Ratio Alchemy, shooting editorial work, portraits, and live music around the Pacific Northwest. I also make pixel art. Both live in Creative.

Personal interests

Away from the desk I geocache, hike in the Cascades, and tinker with whatever technology has my attention that month. I try to stay a lifelong learner — it's the simplest explanation for a résumé that refuses to pick a single lane.

Why "JakobFinci"? The username is a small joke, not a claim. Jakob Finci is a leader of Bosnia and Herzegovina's Jewish community; we're both Jewish, and that's about where the resemblance ends. When I made my GitHub account I wanted something odd and memorable, the Balkans happened to be on my mind, so I searched "Balkan Jews" and the name stuck. That's the whole story — a fond, meaningless nod to a fellow Jew.
