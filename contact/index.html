About Me

Hello! I'm Elias (Eliyahu) Suskind, a passionate creator and problem-solver. With a strong foundation in both technical and creative disciplines, I specialize in combining hardware, software, and machine learning to craft innovative solutions.

My academic journey has taken me through product development at Babson, Electrical and Computer Engineering at Olin, and now Biological Sciences at ASU as I prepare for graduate studies in CS or ECE. I'm also passionate about the arts, especially photography and music.

In my free time, I enjoy geocaching, hiking in the Cascades, and experimenting with new technologies.

Why "JakobFinci"?

Jakob Finci is the current president of Bosnia and Herzegovina's Jewish Community. When I first made my GitHub account, I searched for a unique name and stumbled onto this one while reading about Balkan history.
